<div class="home-bio" markdown="1">

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Princeton University's <a href="https://csdp.princeton.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Center for the Study of Democratic Politics</a>, affiliated with the <a href="https://polracelab.com/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lab on Politics, Race &amp; Experimental Methods</a>. I am also a Postdoctoral Research Affiliate at the <a href="https://campuspress.yale.edu/yaleicl/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Identity &amp; Conflict Lab</a> at Yale University. During my doctoral studies, I worked as a Senior Researcher at Princeton University (2022–2025). I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from Rutgers University in 2025.

I study when and how group identities, particularly those organized around race, ethnicity, and immigration, shape the boundaries of democratic inclusion in diverse societies, focusing on citizens’ perceptions of democracy, commitments to democratic principles and practices, political judgment and decision-making, and intergroup relations. My work primarily uses survey and experimental methods, along with quantitative text analysis, geospatial analysis, and qualitative research.

Prior to my Ph.D., I completed master's degrees in Political Theory at the London School of Economics and in Philosophy at Middle East Technical University.

</div>
